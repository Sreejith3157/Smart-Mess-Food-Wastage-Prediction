Reading these results

Model performance depends on dataset quality and size. A small, noisy or unrepresentative dataset gives unreliable scores and predictions; more clean records from your own mess generally help.

  • All metrics are computed on the {{ metrics.n_test }} held-out test rows that the saved model never saw during training.
  • A large gap between training R² and test R² suggests overfitting. Compare MAE with the baseline MAE: the model is only useful if it is clearly lower.
  • With few test rows, a single random split can be optimistic or pessimistic; the cross-validated R² gives a second opinion.
  • Predictions for inputs far from the training ranges are extrapolations and should be treated with caution.
